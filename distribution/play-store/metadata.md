# Google Play Store Listing & Production Metadata for Lumify

## App Title
**Lumify: Pro RAW Photo Editor**

## Short Description (80 Chars Max)
Studio RAW image editor with tone curves, selective masks, 1:1 loupe & 3D LUTs.

## Full Description
**Lumify** brings desktop-class darkroom post-processing to Android. Engineered specifically for professional photographers and mobile enthusiasts, Lumify delivers a zero-latency, high-precision editing engine that works directly on your device.

### 🌟 Native RAW & Multi-Format Engine
- **Direct RAW Processing**: Native support for DSLR/Mirrorless RAW formats including Canon (.CR2, .CR3), Nikon (.NEF), Sony (.ARW), Fujifilm (.RAF), Olympus (.ORF), Panasonic (.RW2), and universal Adobe DNG (.DNG).
- **Zero Loss Pipeline**: 32-bit floating-point color accuracy preserving maximum highlight recovery, shadow dynamic range, and detail.
- **Universal Format Support**: Edit JPEG, PNG, WEBP, and BMP with equal finesse.

### 🎛️ Studio-Level Pro Controls (Phase 2)
- **Interactive Tone Curves**: Full 2D coordinate spline canvas with draggable control points for Master RGB, Red, Green, and Blue channels with live real-time preview.
- **White Balance Pipette (Eyedropper)**: Tap any neutral gray or white point in your scene to automatically calculate exact color temperature and tint compensation.
- **Selective Masking**: Apply targeted localized edits using Linear Gradient masks (darken skies, sunset warmth) and Radial Gradient masks (spotlight vignettes, facial relighting).
- **Optics & Lens Correction**: Correct optical barrel/pincushion lens distortion and remove purple/green chromatic aberration defringing.

### 🔍 1:1 Precision & Workflow (Phase 3)
- **1:1 Deep Zoom Pixel Loupe**: A floating circular magnifying reticle under your finger to verify tack-sharp focus and inspect high-ISO noise.
- **Batch Recipe Copy & Paste**: Copy your complete tonal recipe and paste it across multiple photos with one tap.
- **Custom 3D LUT Support**: Import standard 3D `.cube` color lookup tables for cinematic color grading.

### 🔒 100% On-Device & Privacy-Respecting
- No accounts, no sign-ins, no subscriptions, and no cloud uploads.
- Your photos never leave your device.
- Full EXIF and photographic metadata preservation on export.
